At Devdaha English Boarding Secondary School, we are committed to building a learning culture where academic growth, character, creativity, confidence and service develop together.

Our Vision

Our vision is “Excellence in Education, Character and Leadership.”

We believe education should do more than transfer knowledge. It should shape responsible choices, develop confidence and leadership, encourage curiosity and creativity, and inspire learners to use their abilities for the good of others.

Our commitment

We are committed to helping every student flourish.

Every learner deserves a safe, caring and stimulating environment in which talents are recognized, ideas are valued and character is strengthened.

What we stand for

  • ✓Providing quality and meaningful education.
  • ✓Developing students’ knowledge, skills and creativity.
  • ✓Promoting critical thinking, innovation and problem-solving abilities.
  • ✓Nurturing discipline, integrity, respect and social responsibility.
  • ✓Providing a safe, caring and child-friendly learning environment.
  • ✓Integrating technology and modern teaching and learning practices.
  • ✓Encouraging sports, arts, culture and extra-curricular activities.
  • ✓Identifying and nurturing the unique talents of every student.
  • ✓Preparing learners to face the challenges and opportunities of the 21st century.
Every learner. Every possibility.A balanced education brings together classroom learning, creativity, teamwork, culture, sport, technology and service.
