About Devdaha E.B.S.S. School

Rooted in values.
Focused on the future.

Devdaha English Boarding Secondary School is a private educational institution located in Devdaha-7, Rupandehi, Lumbini Province. With a long history of serving the local community, the school is dedicated to providing quality education in a safe, friendly, and supportive environment. We welcome students from Nursery to Grade 10 and focus on both academic learning and the overall development of every child. Our school encourages students to learn with curiosity, develop creativity, build confidence, and grow with strong moral values. Along with regular classroom education, we provide opportunities to participate in sports, extracurricular activities, cultural programs, and various school events. We believe that every student has unique abilities and potential, and our goal is to provide the guidance, opportunities, and encouragement they need to achieve their dreams.

Our Story

From a local school to a wider vision for learning.

2053 B.S. / 1996 A.D.

Devdaha English Boarding Secondary School was established in 2053 B.S. (1996 A.D.), beginning its journey with a commitment to providing quality education to students. Since its establishment, the school has continued to grow and develop with the changing needs of education and the community. Over the years, we have focused on creating a supportive learning environment where students can build knowledge, confidence, discipline, creativity, and good character.

Growing with Devdaha

Devdaha English Boarding Secondary School is proud to be a part of the growing Devdaha community. We are committed to providing quality education and creating a positive environment where students can learn, grow, and achieve their goals. Along with academic learning, we encourage students to take part in sports, cultural activities, social programs, and other opportunities that help them develop confidence and good character. With the support of parents, teachers, and the local community, we continue to work together for the better education and brighter future of our students and Devdaha.

Learning beyond the classroom

we provide students with opportunities to learn and grow beyond the classroom. We encourage active participation in sports, extracurricular activities, cultural programs, creative activities, and various school events. These experiences help students discover their talents, develop confidence, improve communication and teamwork skills, and build leadership qualities. Along with academic learning, we focus on the overall development of every student, helping them become disciplined, confident, responsible, and well-rounded individuals prepared for the future.

School Life

More than lessons on a timetable.

School & Facilities

We provide a comfortable and supportive environment where students can learn, explore, and grow. We have well-managed classrooms that make learning interactive and engaging, along with spaces that encourage reading, self-study, and a love for knowledge. We use technology to support modern learning and provide opportunities for teamwork, creativity, and practical activities. We also encourage students to take part in sports, extracurricular activities, and other school programs, helping them enjoy a balanced and meaningful school life.

Sports & Fitness

We believe that physical activity is an important part of a student’s overall development. We encourage our students to take part in sports and fitness activities that help them stay healthy, active, and energetic. Through sports, students learn the importance of teamwork, discipline, cooperation, leadership, and fair play. These activities also help them build confidence, develop a positive attitude, and create healthy habits that can benefit them throughout their lives.

Creativity & ECA

We believe that learning becomes more meaningful when students have the freedom to explore their interests and express their creativity. We provide regular opportunities for students to participate in extracurricular activities, clubs, creative programs, and practical projects. Through these activities, we encourage students to discover their talents, develop new skills, work with others, and gain confidence. We also help our students turn their ideas into action through hands-on experiences that support both personal growth and practical learning.

Community & Leadership

We believe school life is not only about studying, but also about learning how to communicate, take responsibility, work with others, and contribute to the community. We encourage our students to participate in school activities, social programs, teamwork, and leadership opportunities that help them become confident and responsible individuals. Through these experiences, we develop communication skills, leadership qualities, respect, cooperation, and a sense of service. We aim to inspire every student to become a positive member of the community and make a meaningful difference in the world around them.
