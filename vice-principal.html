A message for our students

Growing through learning and responsibility.

“Every day at school is an opportunity to learn something new, strengthen a good habit and become a little more confident in what you can do.”
Dear Students, Parents, Guardians, and Well-Wishers,

It is my great pleasure and privilege to welcome you to Devdaha English Boarding School, an institution with a long-standing commitment to quality education, character development, and the overall growth of young learners.

Having been associated with this school since its establishment, I have had the unique opportunity to witness its journey from its early beginnings to the institution it is today. Over the years, I have seen generations of students grow, learn, achieve their dreams, and become responsible members of society. This long and rewarding journey has strengthened my belief that education is not simply about academic success; it is about shaping individuals with knowledge, character, confidence, discipline, and a sense of responsibility.

At Devdaha English Boarding School, we strive to create a learning environment where every child is respected, encouraged, and given opportunities to discover and develop his or her potential. Along with academic excellence, we give importance to discipline, moral values, creativity, leadership, communication skills, teamwork, and participation in co-curricular and extracurricular activities.

Our dedicated teachers and staff work together to provide students with meaningful learning experiences and a supportive school environment. We also recognize the invaluable role of parents and guardians in the educational journey of every child. Strong cooperation among the school, family, and community is essential for nurturing confident, compassionate, and responsible citizens.

As we continue our journey forward, we remain committed to preserving the values and traditions that have guided our school while embracing new ideas, modern educational practices, and emerging opportunities. Our goal is to prepare students not only to succeed in examinations and careers, but also to face life with courage, integrity, wisdom, and confidence.

I am deeply grateful to all our students, parents, teachers, staff, and well-wishers whose continuous support has contributed to the growth and success of Devdaha English Boarding School.

Let us continue to work together to build a brighter future for our children and to make education a meaningful journey of knowledge, character, discovery, and excellence.

Warm regards,
