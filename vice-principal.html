A message for our students

Growing through learning and responsibility.

“Every day at school is an opportunity to learn something new, strengthen a good habit and become a little more confident in what you can do.”

Education becomes meaningful when students feel encouraged to participate, ask questions, make progress and learn from experience. Our role as educators is to create the guidance and support that helps students take those steps with confidence.

At Devdaha English Boarding Secondary School, we value discipline with understanding, achievement with humility, and learning with purpose. Students are encouraged to respect one another, work together and take responsibility for the choices they make.

School life is more than lessons and examinations. Activities, sports, creative work, leadership opportunities and everyday interactions with teachers and friends all help shape a student's character. Together, these experiences prepare young people to face new challenges thoughtfully and contribute positively to their community.

LearnBuild strong foundations and stay curious.
LeadTake responsibility and set a positive example.
GrowDevelop confidence, respect and resilience.
