School Leadership

A message from the Principal

A strong school is built through purposeful teaching, supportive relationships and opportunities for students to discover what they can do. The Principal’s message reflects that shared commitment.

School Leadership · Principal
PrincipalDevdaha English Boarding Secondary School
Principal

Leading with purpose.

“Learning becomes meaningful when students are encouraged to ask, practise, reflect and take responsibility for what they do.”

We want students to leave school not only knowing more, but also thinking more clearly, working with others, taking responsibility and contributing positively to society.

Our classrooms, activities, sports, projects and school events all play a role in that journey. Students should have opportunities to ask questions, practise new skills, receive guidance and discover what they can do.

Teachers are central to this process. We believe in high expectations paired with encouragement, academic ambition paired with care, and clear routines paired with creativity.
